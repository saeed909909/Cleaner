    How it works
    
    
    It plays one game: guess the next letter. It sees 64 letters of the book, guesses the 65th, and nudges its numbers when it's wrong. That's the same game GPT-1 was trained on, with words broken into pieces instead of letters. Loss measures how surprised it is by the real next letter: 4.56 means pure guessing among 96 characters, and lower is better. It has 2 transformer layers, 4 attention heads and about 110,000 parameters, roughly 1,000 times smaller than GPT-1. Progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and continue later.
